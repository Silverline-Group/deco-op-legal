Privacy Policy

Deco OP · Deco Materials LLC · Effective October 9, 2026

Deco OP (the "Software") is an internal operations application operated by Deco Materials LLC, 2160 N Central Rd Ste 108, Fort Lee, NJ 07024 ("Deco Materials", "we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Software handles and how it is protected. The Software is used only by Deco Materials' authorized staff and partners; it is not a consumer service.

1. Data we process

  • User accounts: name, work email address, role and sign-in activity of authorized Users.
  • Business records: purchase orders, inventory, shipments, customer orders and related documents belonging to Deco Materials.
  • Accounting data from QuickBooks Online: with Deco Materials' authorization through Intuit's OAuth 2.0, the Software reads and writes accounting records (bills, invoices, payments, vendors, customers, items) of Deco Materials' own QuickBooks Online company. We never see or store the QuickBooks password; we store only the access tokens Intuit issues, which can be revoked by Deco Materials at any time.
  • Documents from Dropbox: where connected, business documents (proforma invoices, packing lists, bills of lading) from Deco Materials' own Dropbox folders.

2. How we use it

Solely to run Deco Materials' purchasing, inventory, sales and accounting operations. We do not sell, rent or share data with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to Deco Materials' business.

3. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • All data is stored on servers owned and controlled by Deco Materials (currently on-premises in the United States).
  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Remote access is only possible over a private, authenticated network connection; the Software has no public internet endpoint.
  • Access is restricted to authorized Users with individual accounts and role-based permissions. Every change is recorded in an audit log.
  • Credentials and API tokens are stored in server configuration outside of source control. Encrypted backups are kept for 30 days.

4. Third parties

Data is exchanged only with services Deco Materials has itself authorized: Intuit (QuickBooks Online) and Dropbox. Each exchange is initiated by our server over an authenticated, encrypted connection. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking in the Software.

5. Retention and deletion

Business and accounting records are retained as required for Deco Materials' operations and legal obligations. User accounts are deactivated when access ends. Deco Materials can disconnect QuickBooks Online or Dropbox at any time from within the Software or from the provider's own settings, which stops all further data exchange.
